Our focus

Moo-vement for a Change is a youth-led nonprofit dedicated to improving the lives of animals while advancing environmental sustainability. We focus on practical, student-driven ways to make compassion not only visible, but measurable in our communities.

Education

Animal welfare

We support sanctuaries and raise awareness so animals are treated with compassion and dignity.

Fundraising

Environmental sustainability

We highlight how factory farming impacts land, water, and climate—and why kinder systems matter.

Community

Youth-led action

We make it easy for students to fundraise, educate, and organize in their communities.

Recent work

We’ve organized local donation drives and supported animal sanctuaries through direct giving and fundraising. Every project is built to be achievable for students and meaningful for animals.
