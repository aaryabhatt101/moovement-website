Our mission

As inhabitants of Earth, we share a responsibility to protect the animals who share our home. They rely on us for safety, care, and kindness—so we turn empathy into action.

We focus on the intersection of animal welfare and environmental sustainability, especially around factory farming and food systems.

Core values

  • Compassion with follow-through
  • Student leadership & community partnership
  • Solutions that help animals and the planet

What we do

Awareness

We start conversations that help people understand why animal welfare and sustainability matter—and what choices can help.

Fundraising

We run donation drives and fundraisers to support local humane societies and animal sanctuaries.

Advocacy

We encourage students to learn, speak up, and support practical reforms that reduce harm in industrial farming.

Impact so far

Donation drives

We’ve organized donation drives for our local humane society and supported sanctuaries in the U.S. and India.

Growing globally

Next up: expanding support to sanctuaries in Africa and building more student-led chapters.

Want to partner or sponsor a drive? Email us and we’ll respond with ideas, timelines, and materials.
