Our story

Moo-vement for a Change began with a simple question: if we care about animals, what does action look like? We’re building a movement that empowers young people to advocate for animals and the environment through education, fundraising, and community leadership.

Our belief

As inhabitants of Earth, we have a responsibility to care for the animals who share our home. They rely on us for protection, affection, and kindness—and we choose to accept that obligation with empathy.

“Let us ensure that each animal is treated well—through the choices we make and the systems we shape.”

Why sustainability belongs here

Animal welfare and environmental sustainability are deeply connected. Industrialized farming affects animals, land, water, and climate—so we advocate for solutions that are ethical for animals and healthier for the planet.

  • Student-friendly education and outreach
  • Fundraisers that support sanctuaries and local partners
  • Community conversations about factory farming and food choices
