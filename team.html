Leadership

We’re a student-led team committed to building a kinder world for animals and a healthier planet for everyone.

Founder & Executive Director

Aarya Bhatt

Aarya founded Moo-vement for a Change from a lifelong love for animals and a commitment to ethical, sustainable food systems. As a high school senior, she leads youth-driven education, fundraising, and advocacy.

Director of Strategy

Helene Sargent

Helene helps shape the organization’s vision and long-term direction. As a high school senior, she focuses on building sustainable initiatives that make advocacy approachable and effective.

Director of Operations

Kourtnee Henderson

Kourtnee supports the coordination and execution of projects—from donation drives to outreach. As a high school junior, she keeps initiatives organized so ideas become meaningful action.
